Slow Cooker Ribs


Ribs Stock photos by Vecteezy

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 8 Hour 30 minutes

Ingridients

  • 2 racks of baby back ribs.
  • Woodfire garlic Kinder seasoning.
  • 3 cups of BBQ Sauce of your choice.

Instructions

  1. Pull the membrane off the back of your ribs using a paper towel to help grip the membrane.

  2. Take your Kinder seasoning and sprinkle it generously over the top, sides, & back of your ribs. Be sure to pat the seasoning down to help it stick.

  3. Cut the ribs so they fit into your crockpot. lay down one half then pour 1 cup of BBQ sauce. Then put down the other half and pour the other half of the BBQ sauce.

  4. Once your ribs are in and coated cover with the lid and put the temperature on low and set the timer for 8 hours.

  5. After 8 hours you will have perfectly cooked, fall off the bone ribs. top with more BBQ sauce and enjoy!

    6. Serve and Enjoy!

Spaghetti & Meatballs.


Photo by fatemeh habibyar on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 50 minutes

Ingridients

  • Pasta of your choice.
  • Pasta sauce of your choice.
  • 2lbs ground beef.
  • 1/2 cup crushed pork rinds.
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce.
  • 1-2 eggs.
  • 1 tsp salt.
  • 2 tsp black pepper.
  • 2 tbsp garlic powder.
  • 1 packet of onion soup mix.
  • 2 tbsp italian seasoning.

Instructions

  1. in a large bowl combine ground beef, egg(s), pork rinds, marinara sauce, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion soup mix, and italian seasoning.

  2. Once combined line a baking tray with parchment paper or tin foil. take about 2 tbsps of meat and roll into a ball then Place onto baking try.

  3. Put meatballs into oven at 350°F and bake for 20-30 minute or until golden brown or internal temperature reaches 160°F.

  4. While meatballs are cooking fill a pot with water and bring to a boil. Once boiling add in your pasta and cook to desired tenderness.

  5. Pour most of the pasta water out but leave a little bit in and add your pasta sauce and meatballs.

Serve and Enjoy!

Chicken Soup.


Image by chandlervid85 on Magnific

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 30 Minutes

Ingridients

  • 1lb of cooked, shredded chicken.
  • 2 boxes of chicken broth.
  • 2 tsp salt.
  • 1 tsp black pepper.
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder.
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder.
  • 1/2 tsp dried parsley.
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano.
  • 2-3 medium carrots.
  • 2-3 stalks.
  • egg noodles.

Instructions

  1. Cut up your carrots and celery into bit size pieces

  2. in a pot pour in your chicken broth and add in your seasonings. Bring to a boil add in your carrots, celery, and pasta. let cook for 10-15 minutes them add in your chicken.

  3. After your pasta is cooked to your preferred tenderness ladel into a bowl and let cool for 5-10 minutes.

Serve and Enjoy!

Meatloaf.


Photo by Martinet Sinan on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 1 Hour

Ingridients

  • 2lbs ground beef.
  • 2 large egg.
  • 1-1 1/2 cups of bread crumbs.
  • 1 small onion.
  • 1 bell pepper.
  • 2 tbsp garlic powder.
  • 2 tbsp onion powder.
  • 1/2 tbsp salt.
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper.
  • 2 tsp worcestershire sauce.
  • 1/2 cup of brown sugar
  • 1/2 ketchup.

Glaze

  • 1/2 cup of ketchup.
  • 1/2 cup of BBQ sauce.

Instructions

  1. Chop up onion and pepper add to a medium bowl with gorund beef, salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, worcestershire sauce,bread crumbs, egg, brown sugar, and ketchup. Combine well.

  2. Combine ketchup and BBQ sauce for the glaze.

  3. grease a large loaf pan and tranfer mixture in. Form to pan and put in 375°F oven for 40 minutes then add glaze. bake for another 10 minutes or until internal temperature is 160°F.

  4. Let cool for 10 minutes then serve.

Serve and Enjoy!
