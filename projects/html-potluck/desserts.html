Red Velvet Cake


Photo by amirali mirhashemian on Unsplash

Author:Anonymous Total Time: 50 minutes

Cake Ingridients

  • 2oz red food coloring.
  • 3 tbsp Nestle's Quik chocolate.
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar.
  • 1/2 cup of butter.
  • 2 eggs.
  • 2 1/4 cups flour.
  • pinch of salt.
  • 1 cup of buttermilk.
  • 1 tsp vinegar.
  • 1 tsp baking soda.
  • 1 tsp vanilla.

Frosting Ingridients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter.
  • 8oz cream cheese.
  • 1 tsp vanilla.
  • 1/4 tsp salt.
  • 4 cups powdered sugar.
  • 1 cup of heavy whipping cream.

Cake Instructions

  1. In a small bowl mix food coloring and Nestle's Quick chocolate until a very thin consistency is achived then set aside.

  2. Cream together sugar and butter then add eggs. Once combined pour in thin paste previoiusly made.

  3. In a seperate bowl combine flour and salt and sift 3 times. slowly add to the batter, beatting with a mixer on low or medium. alternating with buttermilk.

  4. After everything is combined hand stir vinegar, then baking soda, & finally vanilla.

  5. Grease two 8" cake pans and divide batter evenly. Bake on 350°F for 30-35 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Let cakes cool for 15-20 minutes then remove from pan and complete cooling on a wire rack.

Frosting Instructions

  1. In a bowl beat butter and cream cheese together until creamy and lump free

  2. stir in vanilla and salt.

  3. In a seperate bowl beat heavy whipping cream until still peaks. Set aside

  4. Using a mixer on low, slowly add in powdered sugar to the cream cheese mixture. Once fully combined flod in the heavy whipping cream.

  5. Once cakes are cooled all the way you are ready to frost and stack them.

    6. Serve and Enjoy!

Sugar Cookies.


Photo by Cody Chan on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 2 Hours 20 minutes

Ingridients

  • 1 1/2 Cups powdered sugar.
  • 1 cup Margarin or butter.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1 tsp vanilla.
  • 1/2 tsp almond extract.
  • 2 1/2 cups of flour.
  • 1 tsp baking soda.
  • 2 tsp cream of tartar.

Instructions

  1. in small bowl combine butter, powdered sugar, egg, vanilla, and almond extract then Set aside.

  2. In a large bowl combine flour, baking soda and cream of tartar. Add in the previous mixture.

  3. Once everything is well combined cover and refrigerate for atleast 2 hours.

  4. Once dough has been chilled divide it in half and roll out to 3/16" thick on a lightly floured surface then use cookie cutters to get your desired shapes.

  5. Place on baking traying and bake at 375°F for 7-8 minutes.
  6. Once baked let cool for 5-10 minutes them move to wire rack to finish cooling. Once cooled you can decorate.

Serve and Enjoy!

Cinnamon Rolls.


Photo by Yosep Sugiarto on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 30 Minutes

Dough Ingridients

  • 3 cups all purpose flour.
  • 1 cup of warm milk.
  • 1 tbsp instant dry yeast.
  • 2 tbsps White granulated sugar.
  • 3 tbsps salted butter.
  • 1 tsp salt.
  • 1 large egg.

Filling Ingridients

  • 1/2 cup salted butter.
  • 1 cup brown sugar.
  • 2 tbsps cinnamon.

Glaze Ingridients

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream.
  • 2-3 tsp clear vanilla extract.
  • 2-3 cups of powdered sugar.

Filling Instructions

  1. In a bowl combine brown sugar and cinnamon then set aside.

  2. melt butter when you are ready to put in the filling.

Dough Instructions

  1. In a bowl combine warm milk, sugar, and yeast. Set aside and let the yeast bloom for 10 minutes.

  2. in a once the yeast blooms, with a mixer, beat in salt, butter, & eggs. Slowly add in the flour.

  3. Once the dough comes together continue to knead it in the mixer while slowly increasing the speed for about 5 minutes. The dough should be tacky but not sticky.

  4. Transfer dough to a greased bowl and let rise for 1 hour.

  5. After an hour lightly grease or flour your surface and roll out the dough into roughly a 12" by 18" rectangle.

  6. Once you have your dough rolled out brush on the melted butter then combine brown sugar and cinnamon in a bowl and sprinkle it onto the melted butter. Lightly press down to make sure everything stays.

  7. Tightly roll up the dough length wise and then cut into 1" slices. Move onto a baking sheat and let rise for 30 minutes to an hour.

  8. After rising bake at 325°F for roughly 14 minutes out until golden brown.

  9. Let them cool for 15-20 minutes before adding the icing.

Glaze Instructions

  1. In a bowl lighly beat heavy whipping cream and then add in vanilla and powdered sugar. Amount may very based off desired thickness of icing.

Serve and Enjoy!

Brownies.


Photo by Molly Keesling on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 1 Hour

Ingridients

  • 1/2 cup butter melted butter.
  • 1 cup sugar.
  • 3/4 cup unsweetend cocoa powder.
  • 2 large eggs.
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract.
  • 1/2 cup of flour.
  • 1/4 tsp salt.
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional).

Instructions

  1. Put melted butter in a medium bowl then add sugar and cocoa powder. Whisk together until fully combined.

  2. Once combined crack in eggs and whisk together and set aside.

  3. Sift in salt and flour and mix until combined. Don't over mix.

  4. Pour into 8x8 greased pan and put into a 375°F oven for around 20 minutes or until toothpick comes out almost clean.
  5. Once brownies are finished baking let them cool for 15-20 minutes before cutting into them and serving.

Serve and Enjoy!
