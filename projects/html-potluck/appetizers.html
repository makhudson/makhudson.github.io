Mozzarella Sticks


Photo by Snappr on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 1 Hour 30 minutes

Ingridients

  • 2 cups of bread crumbs.
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning.
  • 1 tsp dried parsley.
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder.
  • 1 tsp black pepper.
  • 1/2 tsp salt.
  • 2 large eggs.
  • 1/2 cup of flour.
  • 15-20 mozzarella sticks or 1lb mozzarella block.
  • Vegetable oil to fry.
  • Marinara Sauce to dip.

Instructions

  1. In a bowl add in your bread crumbs, italian seasoning, parsley, garlic powder, pepper, & salt. Mix together until combined then set aside.

  2. In another bowl add in your flour and your seasonings, combine then set aside.

  3. Get a another bowl and crack in your eggs and mix together.

  4. Once you have your dry and wet ingredients combined set up your line as, Mozzarella sticks, flour, eggs, then bread crumb.

  5. Dip your mozzarella stick into the flour, tap any excess, then into the eggs, let excess drip off, finally roll into the bread crumbs. After set onto tray or plate.

  6. After you have your mozzarella ssticks fully coated place into the freezer for 30 minutes to an hour.

  7. Once your mozzarella sticks have had time to freeze, pour a shallow amount of oil into a deep pan and heat it to 350°F and then add 2-4 mozzarella sticks (depends how big you pot/pan is) fry for about 1 minute until golden brown. Remove onto a wire rack or plate with a paper towel to cool.

  8. Once all are finished enjoy with some warm Marinara sauce.

Serve and Enjoy!

Banana Bread.


Photo by Priscilla Du Preez 🇨🇦 on Unsplash

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 1 Hour 15 Minutes

Ingridients

  • 2 cups all purpose flour.
  • 1 1/2 cups of mashed bananas.
  • 1 cup of sugar.
  • 1/2 vegetable oil.
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder.
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda.
  • 1/4 tsp salt.
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon.
  • 1/8 ground nutmeg.
  • 2 large beaten eggs.

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, & nutmeg in a medium bowl.

  2. Combine eggs, banana, sugar, & oil in a small bowl.

  3. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients and mix until moist. Do not over mix.

  4. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a 9x5x3 loaf pan and pour mixture into pan. Bake for 55 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean.

  5. Cool in pan for 10-20 minutes then transfer out of the pan onto a wire rack to finish cooling.

Serve and Enjoy!

Fried zucchini.


Dish Stock photos by Vecteezy

Author: Anonymous Total Time: 50 Minutes

Ingridients

  • 1 cup of all purpose flour.
  • 2-3 medium zucchinis.
  • 1 tsp salt.
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper.
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder.
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder.
  • 1/2 tsp dried parsley.
  • 3 large eggs.
  • 1 1/2 cup bread crumbs.
  • 3/4 grated parmesan cheese (optional).
  • neutral oil for frying.

Instructions

  1. Cut your zucchinis into rounds to your preferred thickness. (thin slices are recommended).

  2. In a shallow dish combine flour, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, & pepper.

  3. In another shallow dish beat eggs together

  4. in another shallow dish combine bread crumbs, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, & pepper.

  5. Once everything is combined in their dishes make sure they are lined up as ordered. Dip zucchini in flour, tap off excess, dip in egg, let excessfrip off, then dip in bread crumbs. once coated place on a pan or plate and let chill in the fridge for 15-30 minutes.

  6. While the zucchini chills pour your oil into a pot/pan. heat the oil to 350°F. Fry 3-5 zucchinis for 2-3 minutes per side or until golden brown.

  7. Place finished zucchini onto a wire rack or plate with paper towels to cool.

Serve and Enjoy!
